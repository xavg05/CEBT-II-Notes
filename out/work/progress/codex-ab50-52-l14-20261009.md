Corrections/qualification: dual-reporter attribution dates Elowitz Science 2001 in the PDF; primary paper is 2002. Figures used avoid that caption. Stationarity alone does not imply ergodicity. Noise intrinsic/extrinsic labels depend on boundary: promoter switching is intrinsic to the full cascade even when treated as an extrinsic mRNA input. PAR does not always imply bistability; NAR does not universally suppress all noise. Boolean production step is not differentiated at K; use smooth Hill response. Additional controls and limits are stated rather than claiming every negative-delay loop oscillates. No definite source equation typo found in visually read cascade formulas; protein-noise expression re-derived and equivalent to PDF.
