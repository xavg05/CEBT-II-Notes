## Blind readings and independently derived corrections

Coordinator independently read original p5,23,27,31 without the transcription. Confirmed literal readings on 12 October 2026. Evidence paths: original p005.png,p023.png,p027.png,p031.png in scratch/separations/U2; full-page coordinates.

- p5 denominator letter unclear between g/w, but printed definitions and handwritten gas arrow identify dry-carrier M_g; mass balance independently establishes H=M_vp/[M_g(P−p)].
- p7 H/H* is percentage saturation, not exact conventional relative humidity p/p_sat. Preserve H_R convention and distinguish phi; printed 56% arithmetic is approximate (Python gives55.15%).
- p10 same s at different H is an approximation. Derive exact h balance and constant-enthalpy differential slope −s/[lambda+C_pv(T−T0)]. Treat −s/lambda as dilute/low-sensible-enthalpy approximation.
- p22 rho is dry carrier-gas density, not vapour density, from rho≈PM_g/RT.
- p23 replace erroneous top division by lambda with multiplication; lower handwritten equation and dimensions independently confirm.
- p25 equal driving force at all positions is false for finite continuous gas rate, contradicted by p29. All-constant-regime co-/counter-current times can coincide under same terminal gas states/constant coefficients through an integral change of variable; do not assert local driving force constant.
- p31 use t=integral_w2^w1 [1/r(w)]dw; printed signs corrected and handwritten lower expression reconciled.
- p30 no claim of digitising outside provided chart or obtaining complete source-graph numerical result. Add independently specified numerical quadrature example from verified analytic law, labelled model example.
