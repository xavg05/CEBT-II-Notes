## Derivations and examples

- Filtration: Darcy flux through series resistances, mass of deposited solids proportional to filtrate volume, integration at constant pressure and the empirical cake-compressibility power. Laboratory group is 10102474.9443; pilot areas are 1.15144 and 1.36931 m². Area ratio is 1.18921. Corrected the displayed numerical substitution to use the stated unrounded group. Economic preference for extra area remains conditional.
- Centrifugation: sphere weight minus buoyancy balances Stokes drag; rigid rotation substitutes radial acceleration. Integration of dr/r gives the entry-radius-dependent capture time, and volume/residence time gives conditional throughput. Midpoint time is 13.94735 s, throughput 0.342841 L/min, Sigma 32.41944 m² and doubled-size speed 3535.53 rpm. The midpoint criterion captures 53.68% under a uniform-annular-entry idealisation, not complete clarification. Half-area and full equivalent-area conventions remain distinct.
- Homogenisation: fixed fractional survival per pass gives an exponential, with empirical pressure dependence. The first fit is constrained through the origin, then log slopes are fitted against log pressure. Results: a=2.37419784, k=1.73301182e-7 in the stated pressure units, R²=0.99937084, 4.42546 passes for 80% release and 568.781 kgf/cm² for 70% in two passes. Whole passes require five. Pressure extrapolation and product damage remain qualified. Corrected claims that exponent alone measures toughness or that different pressure/pass combinations necessarily expend identical energy.
- Extraction: two-phase material balance and concentration partition coefficient give Y=Kr/(1+Kr). Recoveries 0.954545 and 0.677419 agree with the delivered examples. Concentration comparisons require an explicit basis. A centrifugal contactor is not automatically a multistage equilibrium extractor.
- Precipitation: subtract the two logarithmic Cohn relations to eliminate the intercept; solve for salt coefficient and operating solubility. Ks=2.04943418 M⁻¹, beta=9.51559836, solubility 80.80291 µg/mL and recovery 55.10950%. Salt targets are 3.23271, 3.57092 and 4.35623 M. The highest exceeds the stated saturation and fitted-data range. Solubility does not imply a universally unfolded protein, and pI is zero net charge rather than absence of charged groups.
- Adsorption: finite equivalent-site equilibrium gives Langmuir saturation; Freundlich remains empirical. Single-batch material balance evaluated at final liquid concentration gives resin volumes 0.159191, 0.214171 and 0.392022 m³. Dynamic packed-bed breakthrough requires a different capacity calculation. Michaelis–Menten normally assumes kinetic steady state, not adsorption equilibrium.
- Chromatography: retention is measured relative to the stated hold-up/void reference; accessible intraparticle volume gives Ve=V0+KpVi. Hydrated gel mass balance gives Vi=64.85294 mL, solid volume 5.14706 mL, peak separation 19.45588 mL, retention ratio 0.4 and separation factor 2.5. Resolution still needs peak widths. Protein A binding depends on antibody species/subclass; FPLC/HPLC labels do not intrinsically fix resolution or throughput.
